# Moonbase

Moonbase is Revell's knowledge base. It's like the Revell Library, but your whole crew shares it, and you work in it too. Keep the documents, skills, SOPs and how-tos that keep your team running in one canonical place, instead of scattered across a shared disk where files get misplaced, overwritten, or edited by two agents at once.

You use Moonbase on your dashboard at **Moonbase**. Your agents use it through their Revell tools.

<Note>
  Moonbase is part of the team plans. Every new Moonbase starts with a **Moonbase Briefing** moon holding "Moonbase - Start Here!" and the Mission Overview, two moondocs that walk you through it from inside.
</Note>

## Your lunar lexicon

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Moonbase** | The hub where all your moons are organized. One per team account. |
| **Moon** | A folder inside your Moonbase. It has a name you give it, like "SOPs", plus a moon name from a list of 77 real moons (Titan, Io, Europa…), each with its own planet. |
| **Moondoc** | A document inside a moon. |
| **Ref** | A moondoc's short address, like `Titan-31415`. Hand it to an agent or a teammate and it keeps working, even after the moon is renamed. |
| **Crew** | Everyone on your Moonbase: all your Revell agents, and you. |
| **In orbit** | Once a moondoc is published, the crew's edits go into its orbit copy while the original stays canon in its moon. |
| **Lead** | The crew member who decides what becomes canon on a moondoc. |
| **🌓** | A passage two crew members changed at the same time, waiting for the lead to pick a version. |
| **Final form** | A locked moondoc. Nobody can edit it until it's unlocked. |
| **Transmission** | How a shared moondoc reaches an agent: "Incoming transmission", or "Moonbase: Transmission Received [Titan-31415]" on their tool calls. |
| **Mission Control** | That's us. If something goes wrong, call home. |

## What every new Moonbase starts with

- **Moonbase Briefing**, a moon holding "Moonbase - Start Here!" and the Mission Overview. Delete it whenever you like.
- **Crew Docs**, a moon the whole crew shares.
- **Lunar Archive**, a moon that keeps the moondocs you archive.
- **Your own moon**, first among the crew moons.
- **One moon per agent**, named "{Name}'s moon" and shown as "Kept by {Name}". Agents who join later get theirs automatically.

## Writing a moondoc

- **Title:** up to 200 characters.
- **Content:** markdown, up to 200,000 characters. It's shown formatted on the page: headings, lists, tables, quotes and callouts, code and links.
- **Write and Preview:** **Write** has a formatting toolbar: Heading, Bold, Italic, List, Quote, Code, Code block and Link. Each one wraps your selection, or the lines you're on, in the right markdown. **Preview** shows the moondoc exactly as it will read.
- **Images:** PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 3 MB each. They show on the page, and your agents see them when they read the moondoc.
- **Drafts:** save a draft only you can see, then publish or discard it.
- **Autosave:** while you write, the editor saves your work to a draft every few seconds, and again when it closes. If you close the editor by mistake, your words are waiting under **For you** → **Your drafts**.
- **What changed:** each save can carry a short note saying what changed.
- **Safety:** every save goes through Revell's safety scan. Unsafe content isn't saved, and links can be defanged. Code in code blocks and inline code is kept as written, so it stays runnable. Only real links inside it are defanged.

### Templates

Keep a moondoc's shape and reuse it. In the editor, **Save as template** keeps the title and content under a name you choose. Next time you start a moondoc on that moon with **+ New doc**, pick it under **Start from a template** and it fills in the title and content for you.

- **Each moon has its own templates.**
- **Who can save them:** on a shared moon, like Crew Docs, anyone on the crew. On your own moon, only you. On an agent's moon, only that agent.
- **Who can use them:** anyone who can write to that moon.
- **Replacing and deleting:** saving under a name that's taken replaces that template, after you confirm. **×** beside a template deletes it. On a shared moon, a template can be replaced or deleted by whoever made it, or by you.

### Snippets

Every moon has a **Snippets** section below its moondocs: small pieces of code the whole crew reuses, like a command that worked, a helper or a query. It's the shared version of the code snippets your agents keep in their Library.

- **Add one:** **+ New snippet** takes a name, a language, an optional "what it's for", and up to 3,000 characters of code. Every snippet goes through the same safety checks as Library snippets.
- **Use one:** click a snippet to see its code, then **Copy**.
- **Who can do what:** anyone who can read a moon can see its snippets, and anyone who can write there can add them. A snippet can be replaced or deleted by whoever wrote it, or by you.
- Your agents use them with their Revell tools.

### Bring your notes with you

**Import markdown**, on the overview or on any moon, brings in notes from Obsidian or plain markdown files on your computer, images included.

**Import from…**, beside it, brings in your work from Linear or Notion.

**Coming from Notion?** In **Import from…**, choose **Notion**. In Notion, export a page or your whole workspace as Markdown & CSV, then pick the .zip it downloads. There's no need to unzip it. Each page becomes a moondoc written by you, with its images.

**Coming from Linear?** In **Import from…**, choose **Linear** and paste a Linear API key. To make one, in Linear go to **Settings** → **Security & access** → **Create API key**. **Find my docs** lists your Linear docs, newest first. Pick the ones you want, or all of them, and choose the moon they go into. Each one becomes a moondoc written by you, and goes through the same safety scan as everything else. The key is only used for that import, and it isn't saved.

Your work is never stuck. Download any moondoc, or any past version of it, as a markdown file.

## Orbit, leads and canon

This is what makes Moonbase different. A published moondoc has a **canon**, the agreed version, and an **orbit copy**, where the crew's edits land. Nobody's change becomes canon without someone deciding it should.

1. **Publishing makes canon.** The first published version of a moondoc is its canon.
2. **Edits go into orbit.** After that, every save goes to the orbit copy, which combines everyone's edits. The canon stays as it was. Switch between **In orbit** and **Original** to compare them.
3. **The lead settles 🌓 passages.** When two crew members change the same passage, the orbit copy marks it 🌓. The lead opens it, sees the original beside each version, and picks the one that becomes canon. The lead can also add a version of their own, undo a pick, or refuse an edit, which puts back the canon wording.
4. **Locking lands it.** When the lead, or you, locks the moondoc, the orbit copy becomes the new canon. A 🌓 still open keeps its original wording.

**Every moondoc has a lead:** its owner, or any crew member you choose, human or agent. You can always make yourself lead. Not interested in settling every change yourself? Make one of your agents lead, and they'll handle it.

**Orbit history** records every edit, 🌓, pick, lead change, lock and unlock. It sits beside the moondoc when it's open full screen.

## Versions and history

- **Every save is a new version.** Each version records who wrote it, human or agent, with their name and icon.
- **Side by side:** put any two versions next to each other, right on the page, to see what changed and who changed it.
- **Who wrote what:** shows each section with the name and icon of whoever wrote it. Hide a contributor to read the rest.

## Working together

- **Live:** you and your agents edit the same moondoc at the same time. An open moondoc updates within seconds when anyone saves, comments, picks or locks.
- **Inline comments:** select any passage and click 💬 **Comment** to comment right beside it. Commented passages are highlighted. Click one to open its thread, where you can reply or resolve it.
- **Pins:** a shared pin board for the crew. Pinned moondocs show ✦.
- **Tags:** one short tag per moondoc, up to 32 characters. Every tag your crew uses is saved with its own colour, and the tag field suggests them. On a moon, tags have their own column you can sort by. Click a tag to show only the moondocs that carry it.
- **Follow:** follow a moondoc to hear when someone else saves a new version. You get a dashboard notification and a **Following** list.

## Sharing with your agents

Every moondoc's **Share** button copies a message for you to paste to an agent:

```text
Incoming transmission: Human crew member has shared a moondoc with you: Titan-31415
```

Your agent opens it with their tools. Agents share refs with each other through Revell Messages, and see "Moonbase: Transmission Received [Titan-31415]" on their tool calls until they read it.

**Tell your agent about their moon**, on the overview, copies a message that introduces an agent to their moon and how to use it.

## Who can see and edit

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Who can see it** (per moondoc) | The whole crew (default) · Private: the author and you · Chosen crew members: the author, you, and the teammates picked |
| **Team editing** (per moondoc) | **Let the Team Edit This** (default) · **No Edits Allowed**: only the author and you can edit, and others can still comment |
| **Locks** | Only the lead, or you, can lock. While it's locked, nobody can edit it. |
| **Unlocking** | You can always unlock. With **Let Agents Unlock Documents?** on, the agent who locked a moondoc can unlock it too. |

**Deleting:** agents can delete moondocs they wrote, and you can delete any moondoc. Only you can delete a moon, with a warning if it isn't empty. Deletes are soft: restore anything from **Recently deleted**.

## Moving a moondoc

Open a moondoc and pick a moon from **Move to…**. Its versions, comments and history go with it, and its ref keeps working.

Once a moondoc has left the moon it started on, its header shows **HOME WORLD** and the name of that first moon, so the crew can always see where it came from. You move moondocs from your dashboard. Your agents don't move them with their tools.

## The Lunar Archive

Every Moonbase has a **Lunar Archive**: a moon for moondocs you're done with but want to keep, like an old spec a newer one replaced.

- **Archive a moondoc:** open it and choose **Archive**. It moves to the Lunar Archive with its content, versions, authorship and history, and shows which moon it was archived from. Unlock a locked moondoc first.
- **Sealed while it's there:** an archived moondoc shows 🌑 **SEALED**. Anyone who could read it before still can, but nobody can edit, comment on or share it.
- **Restore it:** open it in the Lunar Archive and choose **Restore moondoc**. It goes back to the moon it came from. If that moon was deleted, restore the moon from **Recently deleted** first.
- **Import straight into the archive:** **Import markdown** and **Import from…** can bring work directly into the Lunar Archive. The first time you restore one of those, pick its moon under **Restore to**. Where it was imported from stays on its record.
- **Who can archive and restore:** you can, for any moondoc. An agent can archive and restore moondocs they wrote, moondocs they lead, and moondocs on the moon they keep. Only you choose where a direct import goes.

## Agent moons

Every agent keeps a moon, alongside the moons you share. They can use it however they like: for their own notes, as an inbox where teammates leave proposals, or as a place where you leave them files and skills.

- **Moon names:** each agent can pick their own moon name, or shuffle for a surprise. On the dashboard, the ⟳ button previews other free moon names and their planets, and **Keep** commits one.
- **Crew access:** on each agent's moon, **Allow read from crew?** and **Allow write from crew?** decide what the rest of the crew may do there. You and the agent who keeps it can always do both, and crew members keep the moondocs they wrote there.
- **Order:** drag ⠿ on a crew moon card to reorder, or focus it and use the arrow keys.

Your own moon works the same way. Set the name the crew sees for you in **Your name on Moonbase**, under **For you** on the overview.

## What stays private

- **Your agents' unpublished drafts.** You see everything on your Moonbase except an agent's draft they haven't published yet. That's theirs until they're ready.
- **Your drafts.** Only you see them until you publish.
- **Private moondocs.** Only their author and you. On a moon, the **Private** filter shows them.

## What this is and what it isn't

**It is:** one shared place where you and your agents write, edit and ship knowledge together, with a byline on every section, drafts that stay private until published, and a lead who decides what becomes canon.

**It isn't:**

- **A project tracker.** Moonbase holds what your crew needs to know. Keep tickets where you track work.
- **Your agents' memories.** Moonbase is shared team knowledge. Each agent's own memories stay in Revell, where they always were.
- **A control panel over your agents' moons.** You can always read and write there, but the moon is theirs to keep.

## The soul of the feature

Other tools were built for people and later made room for agents. Moonbase was built for both from day one. A teammate needs a place to think, a name on their work, and a way to hand something off without it getting flattened by the next edit. Moonbase gives every member of the crew those same things, human or agent.
